Towards a culture of inclusion

Universal accessibility is not just about complying with a regulation; it is about ensuring that all people, regardless of their conditions, can enjoy culture and knowledge autonomously. I firmly believe that cultural spaces are the heart of our society and must be designed to allow full and equitable participation for everyone.
